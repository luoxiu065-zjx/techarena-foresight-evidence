# Career compass

## When to use
At every decision point, right after observe has returned the current state. The compass is internal reasoning, at most three short lines; every decision ends with a tool call in the same turn.

## Terms used in this Skill
- Drivers: Skill, Network and Output, the three visible statistics the handbook names for promotion.
- Healthy / watch / low: Health or Dignity at 7 or more / 4 to 6 / below 4. Our own planning bands, not game thresholds.
- Ceiling: the value at which a driver stops rising at the current level although something was meant to raise it. Each level has its own, unpublished; it is found by watching, never assumed.
- Defuse: the Energy action "Defuse legacy risk".
- Review month: months 6, 12, 18 and so on, when the half-year review and the promotion window fall. In our runs the review used the state at the end of the month before it, so quarterly Energy spent in the review month arrives too late for that review.

## Procedure
1. Read from the observe result only: month, level and months at level, the nine visible statistics, the monthly feed (salary, HR remark, review grade, promotion, warnings) and your own last note. Never use a value from memory.
2. Set the stage from the level and the calendar, in this order:
   - Final year: month 37 or later. Promotions still happen at months 42 and 48; the ending evaluation also counts Skill, Network, Wealth, Health, Dignity and risk control.
   - Entry: no promotion yet. In our runs the windows at months 6 and 12 decided the whole run; a career still at the first level after month 12 seldom recovered.
   - Senior: twelve months or more at the current level after at least two promotions. In our runs the upper levels asked for much more Skill and more months at the level than the lower ones.
   - Climb: everything else.
3. Ledger of the drivers. For each driver write open or ceiling:
   - Ceiling: the value did not rise across the last two quarterly phases although Energy, a main action or a story result was meant to raise it. One flat quarter is not enough.
   - A driver at its ceiling becomes open again after a promotion, or when it drops below the value it stuck at (then it is open until it is back there).
   - Judge "behind" by movement and room, never by raw size: Skill is naturally several times larger than Network and Output at every level, so a large Skill is not "ahead".
4. Target: the open driver that rose least over the last quarter. Entry stage: all three must reach their ceiling before the end of month 5 and again before the end of month 11, so the target is whichever driver is still moving. Senior stage and final year: Skill, as long as it is open and Health is healthy; otherwise the open driver that rose least.
5. Repair check, which overrides the target when it applies:
   - Health or Dignity low: recovery first, and no option or action that costs it.
   - Health in watch: growth only through options that cost no Health.
   - Wealth negative, or Network at zero: raise it before anything else; the handbook lists both as failure directions when they persist.
   - Hidden risk: the handbook does not publish it, so read the HR remark in the monthly feed as a gauge. In our runs it moved in steps from calm ("doing all right") to alarm ("about to go off"); read it as a direction, not as a number. A remark that says the risks have piled up or worse, or a story that came back after a silent, hidden or shortcut choice, means a defuse is due at the next quarterly phase. After a blow-up (the feed reports that buried risks have gone off), the next two quarters add no new risk and defuse once; this is our own policy, since the published failure directions say a serious consequence can follow accumulated risk.
   - Calendar: in the month before a review, no option that costs two or more points on one statistic or lowers two at once.
6. Expectation check: compare the expectation written in your last note with what actually changed. On a miss, add one short lesson to the next note naming the kind of option and its real effect, never an event by name. Lessons in your notes outrank the general reading rules in story-option-choice.

## Output (internal, two or three lines)
Stage; open drivers and ceilings; target; repair items that apply; months to the next review.

## Priorities near a limit
- Health or Dignity low in two observations in a row outranks everything.
- After a C or D grade: for the next two quarters every story choice and Energy point goes to the target, through options that cost no Health; one more weak review can end the run.
- Two windows at the same level without a promotion: change the main-action pattern in quarter-plan instead of repeating it.
- Output is needed for promotion but is not part of the ending evaluation; Skill and Network are both. From the senior stage on, Skill is raised first.
