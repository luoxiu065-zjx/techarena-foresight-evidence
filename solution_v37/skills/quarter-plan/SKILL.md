# Quarter plan

## When to use
When observe shows the quarterly Energy phase or the main-action choice, after career-compass. The plan is internal, at most three short lines; every decision ends with take_action in the same turn. At the quarterly phase after each half-year review (end of months 6, 12, 18 and so on), load the three Skills again before the first quarterly decision.

## Terms used in this Skill
- Drivers: Skill, Network and Output. Target: the driver career-compass named. Ceiling: a driver that did not rise across two quarterly phases although something was meant to raise it; nothing is spent on it until a promotion or until it drops.
- Healthy / watch / low: Health or Dignity at 7 or more / 4 to 6 / below 4 (our own bands).
- Defuse: the Energy action "Defuse legacy risk". Defuse due (our own schedule, because hidden risk is unpublished and the HR remark in the monthly feed lags): before the first promotion, the quarterly phase after any review that passed without a promotion; from the first promotion on, any quarterly phase that would otherwise be the third in a row without a defuse; the phase after an HR remark that the risks have piled up or anything more alarming; the first phase after a blow-up (the feed reports that buried risks have gone off).
- Stage: entry (no promotion yet), climb, senior (twelve months or more at the level after two promotions), final year (month 37 on), as career-compass set it.

## Procedure
1. Read the menu. Its status_updates and energy_cost give the exact effect and price of every action; use them, never memory. The same action may be chosen more than once within the three points.
2. The first take_action note of the phase states, from the ledger only: "<each driver: open or ceiling>; defuse <due or not due>; stage <name>".
3. Energy, in this order:
   1. Recovery to the band: Health low takes Health recovery until it is at least 4; Dignity low takes Mental recovery until it is at least 4. In the first quarterly phase of the run, and in any phase whose next three months include a review month, buy one Health recovery unless Health is already healthy: the review uses the state before the review month, so Health bought later arrives too late.
   2. Defuse legacy risk when a defuse is due.
   3. End of the run: at the end of months 45 and 48, the points left go to Health recovery and Mental recovery, the lower first, up to the top of each scale. Only points still left go on.
   4. Growth: the remaining points go to open drivers, the target first - Network maintenance for Network, Prepare report materials for Output, Skill learning for Skill only while Health is healthy. Senior stage and final year: Skill learning first while Skill is open and Health is healthy. High-intensity work is not used: its menu text lists a Health and Dignity cost and a hidden-risk effect, and Prepare report materials raises Output without them.
   5. All drivers at their ceiling: Health recovery and Mental recovery up to healthy, then a defuse if none this quarter, then Skill learning while Health is healthy. Leave a point unspent only when nothing above can use it; the menu says unused Energy does not carry over.
4. Main action, the first rule that applies:
   1. Health low, the first two quarters after a blow-up, or the HR remark at "piled up" or worse: Lay low and reduce risk.
   2. Dignity low, or Network at zero: Repair a key relationship.
   3. Entry stage: Build a mentorship when Network is the target (its menu effect raises Network most), otherwise Apply for a key project.
   4. Senior stage and final year while Skill is open: Apply for rotation (its menu effect raises Skill and Network); if two windows at this level passed without a promotion while rotation was the main action, Apply for a key project instead.
   5. Only Network open: Build a mentorship. Network and Output at their ceiling, Skill open: Apply for rotation.
   6. Otherwise, in every quarter including the one before a review: Apply for a key project, because its menu effect raises all three drivers at once.
   7. End of month 48: Build a mentorship if Network is open, otherwise Lay low and reduce risk.
5. Every other take_action note in the phase is one sentence: the action, the driver it serves and "expect <statistic> <+n or -n>".

## Wealth
No Energy action or main action raises Wealth directly; it comes from the monthly salary of the level and the half-year bonus, which the grade sets. A Wealth that is flat or negative is therefore answered by the plan above - growth on the target for the next review, no large purchase or loan in story events - and never by skipping recovery or a due defuse.

## Check before each take_action
- Nothing on a driver at its ceiling except Skill learning in step 3.5; no recovery past the top of a scale; no Skill learning or High-intensity work while Health is not healthy.
- A defuse due this phase is in the plan before any growth point.
- Months 45 and 48: recovery before growth.
- After take_action, observe once.
