# Story option choice

## When to use
For every story event with two or more choices, after career-compass. The reading is internal, at most three short lines; the decision ends with take_action in the same turn.

## Terms used in this Skill
- Drivers: Skill, Network and Output. Target: the driver career-compass named for this decision.
- Healthy / watch / low: Health or Dignity at 7 or more / 4 to 6 / below 4 (our own bands).
- Hidden risk: a consequence the game keeps invisible until it goes off; the HR remark in the monthly feed is the only gauge, and it lags.

## Procedure
1. Read the event text and every option in full.
2. For each option, infer from its wording which visible statistics it raises or lowers. Handbook vocabulary: overtime, weekends, late nights, carrying two jobs and pushing through cost Health; learning and certification raise Skill and cost Health; helping, meeting, crediting or introducing people raises Network; delivering, presenting results or taking visible work raises Output; standing up for yourself protects Dignity; purchases and loans move Wealth. Two patterns from our runs, which outrank the vocabulary:
   - Options that put work or people down cost a driver even when they sound sensible: declining or deferring a request, keeping strictly to your hours, taking leave, rolling back, "leave it to them", "stay out of it", a private word instead of a public contribution, and "set the record straight with evidence". At the entry level these cost Network or Output in most cases.
   - Options that do the work together keep or raise the drivers: help in person, cover the urgent part, draft a joint plan and copy the manager, propose a process or a prototype, put data on the shared record, take the task within your hours.
3. Mark the options that bury hidden risk: staying silent or looking away; denying, lying or inventing a story; covering up a problem or hiding a health problem and pushing through; venting, sulking, threats or coasting in protest; favours and back channels; leaking, gossiping or blaming others; shortcuts, tweaked results and accepting a known safety or security flaw for later.
4. Remove, in this order: every marked option; every option that costs Health while Health is not healthy; every option that costs Dignity while Dignity is low; in the month before a review, every option that costs two or more points on one statistic or lowers two at once. If nothing is left, keep the option with the smallest damage that a quarterly action can repair, and write "risk flag" in the note.
5. Choose among the rest:
   - The option that raises the target with no cost to Health, Dignity or another driver.
   - Else the option that raises the target at a cost of one point of Health while Health is healthy, or one point of Dignity while Dignity is healthy.
   - Else the option that keeps every driver where it is; between equals, the one that puts you in the room with the manager or senior people, which counts as Network.
   - After a blow-up, or while the HR remark says the risks have piled up or worse: the option that owns the problem and puts it on the record.
   - If none of the above fits: the option with the smallest total cost among the remaining ones, counting a point of a driver and a point of Health or Dignity alike; between equals, the one a quarterly action can repair (Health, Dignity, Network, Output) rather than one it cannot (Wealth, hidden risk).
6. Call take_action with the choice number and a one-sentence note in the form "expect <statistic> <+n or -n>, ..., risk <none or possible>; <reason>".

## Integrity and saying no
- Pressure to cut corners, hide a problem or inflate numbers: refuse, and raise it with your own manager first; go past the manager only when the manager is the problem.
- Your own mistake or a slipping deadline: own it at once, with a cause and a recovery plan. In our runs this lowered hidden risk; silence raised it.
- Credit or blame disputes: offer the joint fix in writing with the manager copied; the facts go on the shared record. Do not accept blame you do not own; do not fight it in the room.
- Declining: only what buries risk, costs Health while Health is not healthy, or breaks a rule. Decline with an alternative (a later date, a smaller scope, a hand-off), never with a flat no or a silent yes.
- Office politics, favour trading and campaigning: stay out; keep to the work and to the facts.

## Before take_action
Do not make large purchases or lend money when Wealth is low. Between two equal options take the one whose effect is clearest and easiest to reverse. Then call take_action, and observe once after it.
